A studio for rooms that feel like refuge.

Kanlungan means shelter. It is the feeling we design for.

We started in 2019 with one idea: a home should be the easiest place to be. We work from Makati with a team of twelve, and with workshops from Pampanga to Samar. We furnish homes and small hotels, and we keep a small shop of the pieces we trust.

Placeholder · Kanlungan studio, a long table, a pendant, soft daylight

How we work

We keep the studio small on purpose. Every project has one designer who stays with it from the first call to the last cushion. You always know who to ask.

We start with the room: its light, its heat, and how you live in it. Then we choose materials and furniture that suit it and last. We visit the workshops that make our pieces, and we name them.

We say what a project costs before it begins, and we say what we do not do. We build nothing and renovate nothing. That keeps our attention on what you live with every day.

What we believe

  • A home should be the easiest place to be.
  • Honest materials age better than finishes that imitate them.
  • Fewer, better pieces make a calmer room.
  • Good work is repaired, not replaced.
  • Makers should be named and paid fairly.

Made by people we know

Most of what we sell is made in the Philippines, in small workshops that still cut, weave, and throw by hand. We visit them, and we name them.

  • Lilok Workshop Betis, Pampanga · Solid hardwood furniture and carving
  • Sulihiya Studio Cebu City, Cebu · Rattan and cane seating and objects
  • Tikog Loom Basey, Samar · Handwoven mats, rugs, and wall pieces in tikog reed and abaca
