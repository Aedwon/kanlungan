What we do not do

  • No construction, renovation, or demolition
  • No built-in cabinetry, plumbing, or electrical work
  • No architectural design

We start once the space is built. This keeps our attention on what you live with every day: the furniture, the light, and the feel of the room.

Questions we are often asked

How much does it cost?

Furnishing projects start at ₱1,500,000, including the design fee. The design fee is fixed and quoted per project, based on floor area and scope. The furnishing budget is separate. You pay listed prices for the pieces, and we add no hidden markup on shop items.

How long does a project take?

Most projects take 12 to 20 weeks from signed proposal to handover. Made-to-order lead times set most of that time. Installation usually takes about one week on site.

How do payments work?

You pay 50 percent of the design fee when you sign and 50 percent when you approve the design. You pay 70 percent of the furnishing budget before we order and 30 percent before installation.

Do you work outside Metro Manila?

Yes. We work anywhere in the Philippines. Outside Metro Manila and Calabarzon, the proposal states travel and freight costs.

Can you renovate or build cabinets?

No. We do not build, renovate, or install cabinetry, plumbing, or wiring. The space should be finished before we start.

How many changes can I ask for?

The design stage includes two revision rounds. We settle the layout, materials, and furnishing plan with you before we order anything.

Tell us about your space.

A discovery call takes 30 minutes and costs nothing. Furnishing projects start at ₱1,500,000.
