Layout option 1

Threshold

The site is experienced like walking into a house. A small window of Lilim House opens to the full room, then each section is a room you pass through: a long statement, a pinned gallery, a quiet studio panel, a fan of collections, and a shop you read like a room.

Five layout moves

  1. 01 Window to room. A giant 240px wordmark sits behind a small image window that grows to full bleed and reveals the heading.
  2. 02 Statement as a wall of type. The studio belief is set at 112px across the full width and inks in word by word.
  3. 03 Pinned gallery. Projects pin to the screen while three 70vw panels travel sideways. Without the effect it is a native snap scroller.
  4. 04 Hover rooms and a fan. Service panels reveal a room detail and the starting price. Collections are overlapped portraits that fan out.
  5. 05 Shop the room. One long Lilim House image with six numbered pins, paired with the same six as a numbered list. The product page repeats the window, with a pinned buy panel.

Motion choreography

  1. On load, the hero shows the wordmark and a 36% wide window. The header stays pinned.
  2. On scroll, the hero pins. The window opens to full bleed, the image settles from 1.3x to 1x, and the wordmark sinks behind it.
  3. On scroll, the scrim rises from the bottom, then the heading, text, and buttons rise 40px into place. The hero holds for about 300px, then releases.
  4. On scroll, the 112px statement inks word by word from a pale tint to full text color, each word 1.5% later than the last.
  5. On scroll, Recent projects pins. The panel track moves left by 110vw plus 224px while a clay hairline grows from one third to full.
  6. On hover or focus, a service panel reveals a room image from the bottom edge, text turns light, and the starting price rises into view.
  7. On hover or focus, the three collection portraits move from overlapped to aligned over their names and fan to 4 degrees. The hovered one lifts.
  8. On hover or focus, a pin shows its tooltip card. Hovering a list card lights its pin and shows the tooltip.
  9. On the product page, each gallery image opens from a 9% inset and settles from 1.14x as it enters. The buy panel stays sticky until the gallery ends.
  10. On the product page, Seen in repeats the threshold: a window of the Lilim House room grows to full bleed while a 240px "Seen in" sits behind it. Pairs well with is a snap row.

Fallbacks

  • Without scroll-driven animation, or with reduced motion, every section shows its finished state.
  • Pinned tracks apply only in normal window heights. A very tall viewport, such as a static canvas, shows the natural layout.
  • Mobile uses the same moves with tap and swipe. No content depends on hover.

Reference patterns

  • Scroll-scaled hero window, seen across 2025 architecture site-of-the-day winners.
  • Giant serif wordmark as the first screen.
  • Word-by-word text reveal on scroll.
  • Pinned horizontal project gallery.
  • Layered, overlapping image collage that moves on hover.
  • Editorial shop the look: a large room image with numbered hotspots and a matching product list.
  • Sticky purchase panel beside a long image scroll on furniture stores.
