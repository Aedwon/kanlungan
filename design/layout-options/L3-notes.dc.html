Layout option 3

Light and Shade

Light is the protagonist. Soft shadows of eaves, slatted screens, capiz windows, and palms fall across every page and drift slowly. Calm, layered, and cinematic.

Layout moves

  1. Shadow layer. One reusable overlay of blurred slats and palm fronds, multiplied at low opacity. It sits on the hero, project cards, collections, and product images, and faintly on three page backgrounds.
  2. Extra-large hero type. "Spaces to come home to." at 200px, bottom left, with a sun-position dot on a hairline arc at the right edge.
  3. Pinned statement. The statement stays on the left while three tall atmospheric images pass on the right.
  4. Stacked doors. Project cards pile up with rising offsets. The next card slides over the last. Seen in on the product page uses the same move.
  5. Light on the shop. Collections become a snapping horizontal row. Products sit on a surface band, each with a sharp window-light shape and a cast shadow. The product purchase panel stays free of shadows.

Motion choreography

  1. At rest, the shadow layer drifts 3.5% left and right over 12 seconds. Only transform animates.
  2. On scroll, the hero heading lines wipe out to the right with a stagger, and the intro text lifts.
  3. On scroll, the sun dot travels along its arc from late morning toward evening.
  4. On scroll, section headings reveal with a left-to-right clip wipe as they enter.
  5. On scroll, a progress hairline under the pinned statement grows while the three images pass.
  6. On scroll, each statement image moves 7% against the scroll (parallax inside its frame).
  7. On scroll, each project card pins in turn, settles from a 1.1 zoom, and the next card covers it.
  8. On scroll sideways, the collection images shift 6% inside their frames.
  9. On scroll, product cards rise 56px into place.
  10. On hover, a slatted shadow sweeps across each service panel.
  11. On hover, the light on a product tile shifts, its cast shadow slides, and "View piece →" rises.
  12. With reduced motion, every item above is off. Pinning and snapping remain, since they are layout.

Reference patterns

  • Window and foliage shadows drifting over rooms and products, seen across 2025 and 2026 furniture and interiors winners.
  • Pinned split layouts, with text held on one side while imagery scrolls past.
  • Stacked sticky cards that cover one another, used on architecture portfolios.
  • Horizontal snapping galleries for collections.
  • Oversized serif display type anchored to the bottom of a full-bleed hero.

Cost and risks

  • Blurred, blended overlays cost paint time. Real photography will need the layer tuned per image.
  • Scroll-driven animation works in Chromium and Safari 26. Other browsers show the static state.
  • Shadow never sits under small text. It is masked away from service copy and kept faint on page backgrounds.
