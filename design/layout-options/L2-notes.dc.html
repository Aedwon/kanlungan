Layout option 2

Plan Drawing

The page is built like an architect's floor plan. A visible 12-column hairline grid runs behind everything. Images sit in it like furniture in rooms.

Layout moves

  1. 1Visible grid. Hairline columns in line color run the full page height. Nav items start on column lines. Tiny labels such as "01 · Hero" mark sections.
  2. 2Staggered display words. "Spaces", "to come", "home to." sit on different columns at 156px. A tall image fills columns 8 to 12 and bleeds off the top.
  3. 3Measured stair statement. Each line starts one column further in, closed by a dimension line with end ticks.
  4. 4Project index. Large italic names in rows with location, type, year in columns. A fixed preview column on the right follows the hovered row.
  5. 5Shared edges. Services panels, product cards and details sit exactly on column lines. The sofa details are a drawn front and side elevation with dimension lines.

Motion choreography

All scroll-driven with CSS view timelines. The page at rest is the finished composition. Unsupported browsers and reduced motion show it as is.

  1. On scroll, the hero words drift sideways at different speeds: "Spaces" right, "to come" left faster, "home to." right slower.
  2. On scroll, the hero image parallaxes down inside its frame while the text column rises away.
  3. On scroll, each statement line slides in from the right to its own column, one after another.
  4. On scroll, the dimension line draws left to right with its end ticks.
  5. On scroll, each project name slides in from the left as its row enters.
  6. On hover or focus of a project row, its image replaces the preview. The first project shows by default.
  7. On scroll, the large numerals 1 and 2 rise into place. The tall collection image shifts inside its frame at its own speed.
  8. On scroll, shop cards rise 40px into their cells. On hover, a detail crop crossfades in and "View piece →" slides up.
  9. On the product page, the elevation drawing and its dimension lines draw themselves as they enter view.

Reference patterns

  • Visible hairline structural grid with labels aligned to the columns, seen across 2025 architecture and interiors winners.
  • Huge serif display words distributed across columns, with horizontal drift on scroll.
  • Index lists with hover image previews, common in studio portfolio sites.
  • Technical drawing details with dimension lines, borrowed from architectural sheets.
