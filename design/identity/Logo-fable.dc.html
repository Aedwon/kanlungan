Logo options, round 2

Five new directions, each built on something found in the name itself or in the way the studio works with light and space. Every option shows the primary lockup, the reversed lockup, a real-size header, 32 px and 16 px tiles, and one moment in use.

Lit rooms

We design the inside, so the logo lights the inside of its letters. Every third letter of the name is an n, a small roof on two posts, and each one holds a warm room.

Recommendation

Lit rooms is the strongest: one quiet move that says interior design, reads at every size, animates, and gives the studio and the shop the same mark turned over. Corner comes second for its flexible frame, then Nine, Tuck and Afternoon.
