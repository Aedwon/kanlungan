# Kanlungan

Concept repository for a fictional premium interior design and e-commerce brand created as a portfolio project.

## Locked decisions

- **Brand name:** Kanlungan
- **Business type:** Premium interior design studio with a secondary curated furniture and homeware shop
- **Primary business priority:** Studio first, shop second
- **Market focus:** Premium residential interiors and boutique hospitality
- **Design direction:** Warm modern Filipino
- **Filipino identity:** Subtle rather than overt; expressed through materials, craftsmanship, climate-aware design, spatial sensibility, and project context instead of decorative cultural motifs
- **Brand idea:** Kanlungan should evoke refuge, safety, rest, belonging, and spaces people want to return to
- **Commerce role:** The shop supports the studio brand rather than replacing it; likely categories include furniture, lighting, decor, materials, and curated home objects
- **Portfolio intent:** Demonstrate both high-end editorial/brand design and credible e-commerce functionality using a fictional business
- **Imagery:** Product and project imagery may be AI-generated because this is a demo brand, not an operating business

## Locked service direction

### Full-Service Furnishing

For already-built, largely empty homes, condos, and hospitality spaces. Kanlungan takes the space from unfurnished to move-in or guest-ready without construction work.

Scope may include:

- Furniture
- Lighting and decorative fixtures
- Rugs, curtains, and textiles
- Art and decor
- Appliances and accessories where appropriate
- Sourcing and procurement
- Delivery and installation
- Final styling

This is a core customer-facing service. It is intentionally separate from construction, renovation, or design-build work.

### Design-Only Interior Package

Working customer-facing name: **Interior Design Direction**.

For clients who want Kanlungan to design the space but will handle purchasing, installation, and any contractor work themselves.

Scope may include:

- Space planning and furniture layouts
- Moodboards and material palettes
- Color and lighting direction
- Furniture and fixture recommendations
- 3D renders of key rooms
- Room-by-room specifications
- Curated shopping and sourcing list
- Defined revision rounds

This service provides the design system and visual direction without execution. The concept is locked; the final customer-facing name can still be refined.

## Planning sequence

1. Define Kanlungan's exact service offering.
2. Define the curated shop offering, catalog scope, product taxonomy, and merchandising model.
3. Lock the primary conversion goals, expected to center on consultation booking and shopping.
4. Define the site map, information architecture, page hierarchy, and core user journeys.
5. Build the visual system and design tokens: typography, color, spacing, radii, imagery rules, and motion.
6. Design the homepage and reusable core page templates.
7. Decide technical architecture, e-commerce behavior, and implementation details after the product and design direction are stable.

This order is deliberate: business, content, conversion, and information architecture should be settled before visual tokens and page design.
