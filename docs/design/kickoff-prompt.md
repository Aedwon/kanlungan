# Kickoff prompt for the design session

Paste the text below into a new session opened in this repository.

---

You are designing the website mockups for Kanlungan, a fictional premium interior design studio with a curated shop. This is a portfolio project.

Read `AGENTS.md` first, then `docs/design/design-brief.md`, then every file the brief lists under "Read first." Do not start designing before you have read them.

Do round 1 of the brief only: three distinct visual directions, each shown on the Home page (desktop and mobile) and the Silong Sofa product page (desktop), using the seed content in `docs/content/`.

Show me the three directions side by side as rendered mockups. For each one, give a short name, its main risk, and say which one you recommend. Then stop and wait for my choice.

Rules that matter most:

- Page structure, copy, products, and prices are fixed in the concept files. Layout, type, color, imagery, and motion are yours to propose.
- The studio leads and the shop supports it.
- Filipino identity stays subtle: materials, craft, light, climate. No decorative motifs.
- No lorem ipsum. No real brands or real people.
- Once I choose a direction, keep it exactly as shown.

---
