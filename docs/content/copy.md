# Voice and key copy

Status: **Decided on delegation.**

## Voice rules

- **Calm.** Short, complete sentences. No exclamation marks.
- **Plain.** Common words. Say "wood," not "timber species selection."
- **Specific.** Name the material, the place, and the maker. "Acacia from Pampanga" beats "premium natural materials."
- **Warm.** Write to one person. Use "you" and "we."
- **Quiet about itself.** Do not use "luxury," "bespoke," "elevated," "curated lifestyle," "timeless," or "exquisite."
- **English.** Filipino words appear in product, project, and collection names only. Do not translate them inline.
- Sentence case for headings and buttons. No all-caps running text.
- Numbers: prices as `₱186,000`, sizes in centimeters, areas in "sqm."

## Service page FAQs

Status: **Decided on delegation.** Heading for both pages: "Questions we are often asked." Prices and terms come from `docs/concept/services.md`.

### Full-Service Furnishing

- **How much does it cost?** Furnishing projects start at ₱1,500,000, including the design fee. The design fee is fixed and quoted per project, based on floor area and scope. The furnishing budget is separate. You pay listed prices for the pieces, and we add no hidden markup on shop items.
- **How long does a project take?** Most projects take 12 to 20 weeks from signed proposal to handover. Made-to-order lead times set most of that time. Installation usually takes about one week on site.
- **How do payments work?** You pay 50 percent of the design fee when you sign and 50 percent when you approve the design. You pay 70 percent of the furnishing budget before we order and 30 percent before installation.
- **Do you work outside Metro Manila?** Yes. We work anywhere in the Philippines. Outside Metro Manila and Calabarzon, the proposal states travel and freight costs.
- **Can you renovate or build cabinets?** No. We do not build, renovate, or install cabinetry, plumbing, or wiring. The space should be finished before we start.
- **How many changes can I ask for?** The design stage includes two revision rounds. We settle the layout, materials, and furnishing plan with you before we order anything.

### Interior Design Direction

- **How much does it cost?** From ₱2,500 per square meter. Minimum fee ₱150,000. For a 100 sqm home, the fee is ₱250,000. You pay 50 percent when you sign and 50 percent before we deliver the design book.
- **What do I receive?** You receive a design book with layouts, a moodboard, a material palette, color and lighting direction, renders of key rooms, room-by-room specifications, and a sourcing list. The design stage includes two revision rounds.
- **How long does it take?** Most projects take 5 to 8 weeks. We include one follow-up call within 60 days of delivery.
- **Who buys and installs the pieces?** You do. Our sourcing list draws on the shop first, then on outside sources. We do not order, deliver, or install. We also do not build or manage contractor work. If your space needs that, finish it before we start.
- **Can you work with a home that is far away?** Yes. We deliver this service remotely, anywhere. You send floor plans and photos, or you book a paid site measure if you are in range.
- **Can I move to full furnishing later?** Yes. You can upgrade to Full-Service Furnishing, and we credit the design fee you have already paid.

### Delivery and returns

- **Heading:** Delivery and returns
- **Where we deliver:** We deliver within the Philippines. For orders from other countries, write to us through the contact form.
- **Furniture:** Our crew delivers furniture, places it in your room, and assembles it. Delivery is free in Metro Manila when your furniture totals more than ₱50,000. Otherwise it costs ₱2,500. Outside Metro Manila, we calculate the fee by region at checkout.
- **Small items:** Lighting, cushions, throws, objects, and prints go by courier across the country. Delivery is free when these items total more than ₱10,000. Otherwise it costs ₱350.
- **Timing:** In-stock items ship in 3 to 7 business days. Made-to-order items show their lead time in weeks on the product page. We contact you to schedule furniture delivery when your piece is ready.
- **Returns:** You can return in-stock items within 14 days of delivery if they are unused. Made-to-order and one-of-a-kind pieces cannot be returned, except for defects or damage in transit.
- **If something arrives damaged:** Write to us within 7 days of delivery with a photo. We repair or replace the piece.
- **Warranty:** Kanlungan Originals carry a two-year warranty on furniture construction. Pieces from other makers carry the maker's warranty.

### Care guide

- **Heading:** Care guide
- **Intro:** Natural materials change slowly and look better for it. These habits keep them in good shape.
- **Wood:** Dust with a soft, dry cloth. Wipe spills right away and use coasters and mats. Re-oil oiled finishes once a year, and keep wood out of direct sun and away from vents.
- **Cane and rattan:** Vacuum with a soft brush, or wipe with a damp cloth. Keep cane away from standing water and strong sun. Moderate humidity keeps it supple.
- **Linen and cotton:** Follow the care label. Wash removable covers in cool water and dry them flat or in shade. Linen softens with each wash and wrinkles naturally.
- **Wool rugs:** Vacuum without a beater bar. Blot spills with a clean cloth and do not rub. Rotate the rug twice a year so it wears evenly.
- **Stoneware:** Wipe with a damp cloth and mild soap. Ash glazes may show small variations, which are part of the piece. Do not heat or chill a piece suddenly.
- **Marble:** Wipe with a damp cloth and dry it. Honed marble can stain. Blot spills at once, and avoid acids such as citrus and wine. Use coasters.
- **Brass:** Unlacquered brass darkens and develops a patina over time. Wipe with a dry cloth. To restore shine, use a soft cloth and a little mild soap, then dry well.
