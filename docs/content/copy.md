# Voice and key copy

Status: **Decided on delegation.**

## Voice rules

- **Calm.** Short, complete sentences. No exclamation marks.
- **Plain.** Common words. Say "wood," not "timber species selection."
- **Specific.** Name the material, the place, and the maker. "Acacia from Pampanga" beats "premium natural materials."
- **Warm.** Write to one person. Use "you" and "we."
- **Quiet about itself.** Do not use "luxury," "bespoke," "elevated," "curated lifestyle," "timeless," or "exquisite."
- **English.** Filipino words appear in product, project, and collection names only. Do not translate them inline.
- Sentence case for headings and buttons. No all-caps running text.
- Numbers: prices as `₱186,000`, sizes in centimeters, areas in "sqm."

## Fictional-brand notice

Shown in the footer of every page:

> Kanlungan is a fictional brand created as a portfolio project. Products, makers, projects, and prices are illustrative. Nothing shown is for sale.

The payment step and the order confirmation carry the stronger notice in `docs/concept/conversion.md`.

## Services

### Full-Service Furnishing

- **Promise:** From empty to ready, without you managing a single delivery.
- **Who it is for:** You have a finished, empty home, condo, or small hotel. You want to move in or open once, with everything in place.
- **Short version for cards:** We design, source, deliver, and style every room.

### Interior Design Direction

- **Promise:** A complete design for your space, yours to carry out at your own pace.
- **Who it is for:** You want our design and prefer to buy and install things yourself, or over time.
- **Short version for cards:** We design the space. You bring it to life.

### What we do not do

- **Heading:** What we do not do
- **Text:** We do not build, renovate, or install cabinetry, plumbing, or wiring. We start once the space is built. This keeps our attention on what you live with every day: the furniture, the light, and the feel of the room.
