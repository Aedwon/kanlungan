## 2. Brief

A couple in their thirties. Both work long hours, and one works from home two days a week. This is the first home they own. The developer handed it over bare.

They brought about six hundred records and a turntable, about four hundred books, a television, a ribbed glass lamp from a grandmother's house, a few small pictures, a wool blanket, and a desk chair.

How they live:

- They come home late. Dinner is at the round table, for two, at about 8:30.
- Evenings are in the living room: a record, the screen, or a book.
- The bedroom has no screen. They read there. That is their choice.
- The second bedroom is a study on working days and a guest room when a parent visits.
- Friends come on weekends. Four can eat at the table.

What they asked:

- To move in once, with everything in place.
- The records and books out where they can reach them.
- A home that is lit when it is dark outside.
- Nothing built. The developer's kitchen, wardrobes, and floor stay.

Kanlungan took the unit from handover to move-in in 14 weeks, with 4 days on site.

## 3. Concept

**A lit room above the city: the couple's records, books, and pictures in warm wood against white walls, with lamps on at every seat when the towers turn blue.**

Mood: lit, collected, at ease.

This is the picked image with a design behind it. It keeps what Aedwon chose: a room that is lit, full shelves, the record player, plants, a throw, warm wood against white, a deep sofa, and one leading chair. It corrects what the studio hand lists: the copied lounge chair, the flowering branches, and the generic pieces. It adds the screen he asked for.

The living room and the dining zone are the fullest rooms. The study is a working room. The bedroom is the calmest. No room has bare walls.

### Leading piece in each room

| Room | Leading piece |
|---|---|
| Living | The record console: 280 cm of natural mahogany, made for the wall where the sockets are |
| Dining and kitchen | The round terrazzo table under the paper lantern |
| Main bedroom | The bed, upholstered in sand linen |
| Study | The mahogany desk at the glass |

### The moves

1. **One long console for six hundred records, and for the screen.** It stands on the only wall with sockets. The turntable, the amplifier, and the screen share it.
2. **Books and records in the open.** Open shelving beside the console. Nothing is behind doors except what the developer built.
3. **Their pictures around the screen.** The screen is one dark rectangle among several frames.
4. **Every bulb changed, and a lamp at every seat.** The cool ceiling lights became soft and dimmable. Lamps do the rest. The room stays lit against the dusk.
5. **Linen from the ceiling across each wall of glass.** It softens the glass and makes a west window usable in the afternoon.

One line on the page states that nothing was built or painted.

### How Tanaw differs from the other four

It is the urban one: a compact home with no green outside, photographed mostly in the evening. It is the only project led by natural mahogany, records, and books. Its register row lists what no other project may repeat.

## 6. Palette and materials

Designed for this unit: a cold white box with gray light and no green outside. The room supplies the warmth through what it is made of.

| Role | What | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Pale | White walls (the client's). Oat and white linen. Sand linen. Pale terrazzo. | Sofa, curtains, bed, table top |
| Dark | Blackened steel. The screen. Black-and-white photographs. Record and book spines. Dark-glazed stoneware. | Shelving frames, floor lamp, table base |
| Warm | Natural mahogany, red-brown, in every room. Honey acacia. Aged brass. | Console, chair, low table, desk, bed tables, credenza, shelves, lamps |
| Color | Rust and moss, in small doses. An old rug in faded brick and gray-blue. Indigo in the study. The earth and olive of the painting. | Chair cushions, two sofa cushions, rug, handloom fragments |

Rules for this project:

- One bold thing per room at most. Living: the rust wool chair. Dining: the painting. Study: the indigo fragments. Bedroom: none.
- No furniture matches the gray floor.
- Cane appears once, in one lamp. Rattan does not appear.
- No stone table. The terrazzo table is round, pale, and in the second zone.
- Something on a wall in every room.

No material board is made unless pieces drift between frames.
