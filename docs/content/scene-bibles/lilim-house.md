# Scene bible: Lilim House

Status: **Decided on delegation** on 2026-11-14. Plan drawing: `design/photography/plans/lilim-house.svg` (PNG export beside it). The approved `lilim-hero.png` is the anchor for the living and dining spaces; this bible was drawn to agree with it.

Coordinates are meters. Origin is the southwest inside corner of the great room. x runs east, y runs north. Headings are compass degrees (0 is north, 90 east, 180 south, 270 west).

## Site and light

- Hillside in Antipolo. The valley and the long view are to the **west**. The hill rises to the east, behind the bedroom wing.
- Time for every shot: about 3:30 pm, dry season. Sun from the west-southwest, still high.
- Direct sun reaches only the outer strip of the terrace pavers (x from -4.0 to -3.0). Inside rooms get soft bounced light. No sun patches on interior floors except in `lilim-g04-afternoon-table`, which allows one long, soft band across the table from the west doors.
