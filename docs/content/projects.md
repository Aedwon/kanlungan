## Lilim House

- **Location:** Antipolo, Rizal
- **Type:** Residential, family house
- **Floor area:** 320 sqm
- **Service:** Full-Service Furnishing
- **Year:** 2025
- **Scope:** Living, dining, four bedrooms, covered terrace
- **Brief:** A family of five moved into a newly built hillside house with deep eaves and large openings. They wanted rooms that stay cool and calm and that hold up to children.
- **Approach:** The studio kept furniture low so the views stay open. Cane and rattan let air move through the rooms. Dark wood grounds the bright, shaded light. Washable linen covers and wool rugs handle daily family use.
- **Material palette:** Plantation mahogany, cane, oat linen, wool, honed Romblon marble, unlacquered brass
- **Shop the project:** Silong Sofa, Dapithapon Lounge Chair, Batis Coffee Table, Hapag Dining Table, Hain Dining Chair, Himlay Bed, Aparador Cabinet, Lambot Wool Rug, Kapis Pendant, Tapayan Vessel
- **Image notes:** Shaded daylight, greenery through wide openings, long horizontal views, a terrace with a bench

### Approach, expanded

The house faces the valley and has deep eaves, so the rooms stay shaded and cool. We kept furniture low and left clear sightlines to the garden from the living room, the dining room, and the terrace. Cane panels and open weaves let air move through the house without fans running all day.

A family of five uses every room, so we chose pieces that can take it. Linen covers come off and wash. Wool rugs hide wear. Solid mahogany can be refinished when it marks. We placed the dining table in the middle of the plan because the family eats together there every day.

## Tanaw Residence

- **Location:** Bonifacio Global City, Taguig
- **Type:** Residential, two-bedroom condo
- **Floor area:** 118 sqm
- **Service:** Full-Service Furnishing
- **Year:** 2025
- **Scope:** Living and dining, two bedrooms, study
- **Brief:** A couple received a bare unit on a high floor. Both work long hours. They wanted to move in once, with everything in place.
- **Approach:** One wood tone and one linen run through the whole unit so the small plan reads as one space. Storage is tall and closed. Lighting is low and warm, to contrast with the city outside the glass.
- **Material palette:** Acacia, sand linen, stoneware, brass, opal glass, wool
- **Shop the project:** Silong Sofa (two-seat), Sulok Side Table, Estante Shelving, Baul Credenza, Sinag Floor Lamp, Luad Table Lamp, Tala Wall Light, Hablon Cushion, Amihan No. 3

### Approach, expanded

The unit is 118 sqm, and the plan is compact. We used one wood tone, acacia, and one linen color, sand, in every room. The eye does not stop at a change of material, so the unit reads as one larger space.

Both owners work long hours and come home late. We kept storage tall and closed so surfaces stay clear. Lamps sit low and give a warm light that contrasts with the glass and the skyline. Each room has one place to put a bag, a book, and a glass of water.

## Dalampasigan Inn

- **Location:** San Juan, La Union
- **Type:** Hospitality, 12-room boutique inn
- **Floor area:** 640 sqm
- **Service:** Full-Service Furnishing
- **Year:** 2024
- **Scope:** Twelve guest rooms, lobby, breakfast room
- **Brief:** The owners finished building a small inn near the beach. They needed twelve rooms and two shared spaces ready for guests in one season, with pieces that survive salt air and sand.
- **Approach:** The studio designed one room type and repeated it with small changes in textile color. Furniture is solid and repairable. Reed mats replace wool rugs because they handle sand. The breakfast room has one long shared table.
- **Material palette:** Acacia, tikog reed, rattan, handloomed cotton, stoneware, limewash
- **Shop the project:** Himlay Bed, Bangko Bench, Tuntungan Stool, Banig Rug, Hain Dining Chair, Hapag Dining Table (260 cm), Lenta Pendant, Kumot Throw, Bilao Tray, Habi Wall Piece
- **Image notes:** Bright coastal light, pale walls, open windows, a guest room with a reed mat, a long breakfast table

The owners needed twelve rooms ready in one season. We designed one room and repeated it, changing only the textile color from room to room. This shortened the schedule and made each room easy for staff to prepare and service.

Salt, sand, and heavy use shape every choice. Furniture is solid wood that can be sanded, oiled, and repaired. Tikog reed mats clean easily. In the breakfast room, one long table lets guests sit together or apart.

## Pahinga Villas

- **Location:** Panglao, Bohol
- **Type:** Hospitality, 8-villa resort
- **Floor area:** 520 sqm
- **Service:** Full-Service Furnishing
- **Year:** 2025
- **Scope:** Eight one-bedroom villas, reception pavilion
- **Brief:** The owners built eight villas in a coconut grove a short walk from the beach. They wanted each villa to feel like a private house, not a hotel room, and to stay easy to maintain between guests.
- **Approach:** Each villa has one generous bed, one daybed on the terrace, and very little else. Cane and rattan keep air moving when the doors are open. Linen curtains soften the strong island light. The studio specified one finish and one fabric across all eight villas so any piece can be repaired or replaced from stock.

Each villa has one generous bed, one daybed on the terrace, and very little else. We wanted guests to feel they were in a private house, so the rooms hold only what people use. Doors open to the grove, and cane and rattan keep air moving through the villa.

The owners need the villas to stay easy to run. We specified one finish and one fabric for all eight villas. If a piece wears or breaks, staff can repair it or replace it from a single stock. The reception pavilion uses the same materials, so the property reads as one place.

## Hardin Apartment

- **Location:** Legazpi Village, Makati
- **Type:** Residential, one-bedroom condo
- **Floor area:** 74 sqm
- **Service:** Interior Design Direction
- **Year:** 2026
- **Scope:** Full unit design book, renders of living room and bedroom
- **Brief:** A first-time owner wanted a clear plan to buy toward over a year, starting with a few good pieces.
- **Approach:** The studio delivered a layout, a palette, and a phased sourcing list in three stages. The owner bought the sofa, a lamp, and a rug first. The rest follows the plan.
- **Material palette:** Plantation mahogany, clay linen, cane, stoneware, hinoki
- **Shop the project:** Silong Sofa (two-seat), Kiso Stool, Luad Table Lamp, Lambot Wool Rug (170 × 240), Pasilyo Console Table, Bato Object
- **Image notes:** Mix renders and photographs. Show the design book pages, a moodboard, and a floor plan beside finished-room images. Plants and a small balcony garden.

### Gallery captions

3. Bedroom, render. The planned bedroom in warm light.

### Approach, expanded

The owner was buying a first home and had a budget to spread over a year. We designed the whole unit at once, so every later purchase fits the plan. The design book holds the layout, the palette, and a specification for each room.

The sourcing list has three stages. Stage one is the sofa, a lamp, and a rug, which make the living room usable at once. Stages two and three add the bedroom and the entry. The owner buys when ready, and nothing needs replacing along the way.
