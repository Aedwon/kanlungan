## Lilim House

Revised on 2026-11-16. The design is in [the Lilim design file](project-designs/lilim-house.md). The captions are provisional until the photographs are approved. Field names and the caption format stay as the site expects them.

- **Location:** Antipolo, Rizal
- **Type:** Residential, family house
- **Floor area:** 320 sqm
- **Service:** Full-Service Furnishing
- **Year:** 2025
- **Scope:** Living, dining, kitchen, family room, three bedrooms, covered terrace
- **Duration:** 18 weeks from signed proposal to handover, with 6 days on site
- **As handed over:** A new house, finished by the contractor and empty. White painted walls, pale gray tile, flat white ceilings with recessed downlights, bronze aluminum sliding doors, and a bare bulb at each ceiling point.
- **Brief:** A family of five moved into the first house they built, on a hillside above a green valley. The contractor handed it over white, tiled, and empty. They wanted it calm and finished from the first night, with the view left open and nothing more built.
- **Approach:** The studio kept the great room quiet, so the valley is the color. Charcoal linen, black wood, and steel sit against white linen, pale wool, and gray stone, with cane and warm wood in every room. One slab of Romblon marble leads the living room. The family's books and screen fill a room of their own. Nothing was built or painted.
- **Material palette:** Honed Romblon marble, ebonized plantation mahogany, blackened steel, white and charcoal linen, wool, cane
- **Shop the project:** Silong Sofa (charcoal), Dapithapon Lounge Chair, Lambot Wool Rug, Luad Table Lamp, Sinag Floor Lamp, Hapag Dining Table (ebonized mahogany), Hain Dining Chair (ebonized mahogany), Kapis Pendant, Tapayan Vessel, Yakap Sofa (sand), Baul Credenza (ebonized), Lenta Pendant, Himlay Bed (ebonized mahogany), Aparador Cabinet, Duyan Daybed, Batis Coffee Table
- **Image notes:** A white, shaded living room open to a green valley, with a charcoal sofa, a large stone table, white linen curtains, and lamps lit in the late afternoon

### Approach, expanded

The house looks over a valley, and green fills every opening. We kept the great room quiet, so the valley is the color. Charcoal linen, black wood, and steel sit against white linen, pale wool, and gray Romblon marble. One stone table, made for the house, leads the living room. Cane and warm wood appear in every room.

The builder left cool ceiling lights. We changed every bulb to a warm one and added lamps where the family sits, eats, and reads. White linen runs from the ceiling for the full length of the glass. We left the middle of the great room open, because three children need a floor. Their books, their games, and the screen have a room of their own. Nothing was built or painted.

## Tanaw Residence

Revised on 2026-11-16. The design is in [the Tanaw design file](project-designs/tanaw-residence.md). The captions are provisional until the photographs are approved. Field names and the caption format stay as the site expects them.

- **Location:** Bonifacio Global City, Taguig
- **Type:** Residential, two-bedroom condo
- **Floor area:** 118 sqm
- **Service:** Full-Service Furnishing
- **Year:** 2025
- **Scope:** Living, dining and kitchen, main bedroom, main bath, study and guest room, entry
- **Duration:** 14 weeks from signed proposal to handover, with 4 days on site
- **As handed over:** A bare unit from the developer, clean and empty. White painted walls, wood-look vinyl floors, flat ceilings with recessed downlights, fixed glass facing west, the developer's kitchen and wardrobes, and a bare bulb at each ceiling point.
- **Brief:** A couple received a bare unit on a high floor. Both work long hours. They own six hundred records and four hundred books. They wanted to move in once, with everything in place, and to come home to a room that is lit.
- **Approach:** The studio kept the couple's records, books, and pictures in the open, in warm mahogany against the white walls. One long console, made for the unit, holds the records, the turntable, and the screen. Every seat has a lamp, so the rooms stay lit against the city at dusk. Nothing was built or painted.

### Approach, expanded

The unit is a white box on a high floor, with towers outside and no green. We let the couple's own things warm it. Both owners come home late. The developer left cool ceiling lights. We changed every bulb and put a lamp at every seat, so the rooms are lit when the city turns blue. Oat linen runs from the ceiling across each wall of glass. The second bedroom works as a study and takes a guest. The kitchen, the wardrobes, and the floor are the developer's. Nothing was built or painted.

## Dalampasigan Inn

Revised on 2026-11-16. The design is in [the Dalampasigan design file](project-designs/dalampasigan-inn.md). The captions are provisional until the photographs are approved. Field names and the caption format stay as the site expects them.

- **Location:** San Juan, La Union
- **Type:** Hospitality, 12-room boutique inn
- **Floor area:** 640 sqm
- **Service:** Full-Service Furnishing
- **Year:** 2024
- **Scope:** Twelve guest rooms and baths, lobby, breakfast room, corridors
- **Duration:** 16 weeks from signed proposal to opening, with 9 days on site
- **As handed over:** An older inn, cleared and repainted by its new owners, and empty. White painted walls, cream floor tile, aluminum sliding windows, and a tube light, a ceiling fan, and an air conditioner in each room.
- **Brief:** Two siblings took over an older inn near the surf beach. They needed twelve rooms and two shared rooms ready for guests in one season, with pieces that survive salt air and sand, and rooms that a small staff can prepare the same way every day.
- **Approach:** The studio designed one room and made it twelve times. Rooms that face the sea have no color but the sea. Rooms that face the garden have the same cloth in indigo. Furniture is natural oiled acacia, honey in tone, solid and repairable. Reed mats replace wool rugs because they handle sand. The breakfast room has one long table, made for the room, for the whole inn. Nothing was built or painted.
- **Material palette:** Natural oiled acacia (honey), cane, tikog reed, white linen, handloomed cotton, stoneware, blackened steel
- **Shop the project:** Himlay Bed (acacia), Tuntungan Stool, Bangko Bench, Banig Rug, Kumot Throw, Hain Dining Chair (acacia), Bilao Tray, Habi Wall Piece
- **Image notes:** A plain white guest room in bright morning light, with a honey acacia bed with a cane headboard, white linen, a reed mat, and the sea through one window

9. Breakfast room. In the evening the far end is the common corner, with the inn's one screen.

### Approach, expanded

The owners needed twelve rooms ready in one season. We designed one room and made it twelve times. Eight rooms face the sea, and in those the sea is the only color. Four face the garden, and in those the throw and one cushion are indigo. Every room has the same pieces in the same places, so the staff can prepare any of them the same way. Each room has one drawing, and no two are alike.

Salt, sand, and heavy use shape every choice. The Himlay and the Hain share the honey tone of natural oiled acacia. Furniture is solid wood that can be sanded, oiled, and repaired. Tikog reed mats clean easily. The ceilings belong to the fans, so every lamp we added stands on something. In the breakfast room, one long table lets guests sit together or apart. The walls, the tile, the windows, the baths, and the kitchen are the owners'. Nothing was built or painted.

## Pahinga Villas

- **Location:** Panglao, Bohol
- **Type:** Hospitality, 8-villa resort
- **Floor area:** 520 sqm
- **Service:** Full-Service Furnishing
- **Year:** 2025
- **Scope:** Eight one-bedroom villas, reception pavilion
- **Brief:** The owners built eight villas in a coconut grove a short walk from the beach. They wanted each villa to feel like a private house, not a hotel room, and to stay easy to maintain between guests.
- **Approach:** Each villa has one generous bed, one daybed on the terrace, and very little else. Cane and rattan keep air moving when the doors are open. White linen curtains soften the strong island light. The studio specified ebonized wood and white linen across all eight villas so any piece can be repaired or replaced from stock.
- **Material palette:** Ebonized plantation mahogany, honey rattan and cane, white linen, honed cream limestone, stoneware, capiz shell
- **Shop the project:** Himlay Bed (ebonized mahogany), Duyan Daybed, Dapithapon Lounge Chair, Sulok Side Table (ebonized), Kapis Pendant, Luad Table Lamp, Tabing Linen Curtain, Tapayan Vessel, Habi Wall Piece
- **Image notes:** Filtered light through coconut palms, open sliding doors, a low bed with white linen, a daybed on a shaded terrace, warm lamps at dusk

### Approach, expanded

Each villa has one generous bed, one daybed on the terrace, and very little else. We wanted guests to feel they were in a private house, so the rooms hold only what people use. Doors open to the grove, and cane and rattan keep air moving through the villa.

The owners need the villas to stay easy to run. We specified ebonized wood and white linen for all eight villas. If a piece wears or breaks, staff can repair it or replace it from a single stock. Frames, posts, rafters, and built-ins are ebonized mahogany above honed cream limestone floors. Natural honey rattan stays light against the dark wood. The reception pavilion uses the same materials, so the property reads as one place.

## Hardin Apartment

- **Location:** Legazpi Village, Makati
- **Type:** Residential, one-bedroom condo
- **Floor area:** 74 sqm
- **Service:** Interior Design Direction
- **Year:** 2026
- **Scope:** Full unit design book, renders of living room and bedroom
- **Brief:** A first-time owner wanted a clear plan to buy toward over a year, starting with a few good pieces.
- **Approach:** The studio delivered a layout, a palette, and a phased sourcing list in three stages. The owner bought the sofa, a lamp, and a rug first. Later stages add the oat Unan Bed, Bakal Side Table, and ebonized Pasilyo. Natural mahogany, ebonized mahogany, and hinoki are mixed on purpose.
- **Material palette:** Natural and ebonized plantation mahogany, clay and oat linen, cane, stoneware, hinoki
- **Shop the project:** Silong Sofa (two-seat, clay), Kiso Stool, Luad Table Lamp, Lambot Wool Rug (170 × 240), Pasilyo Console Table (ebonized mahogany), Bato Object, Unan Bed (queen, oat), Bakal Side Table
- **Image notes:** Mix renders and photographs. Show the design book pages, a moodboard, and a floor plan beside finished-room images. Plants and a small balcony garden.

### Gallery captions

2. Living room, photograph. The first three pieces in place: sofa, lamp, and rug.
3. Bedroom, render. The planned queen Unan Bed in oat linen and a Bakal Side Table in warm light.
4. Design book. The cover and contents page.

9. Entry, photograph. The ebonized mahogany Pasilyo Console Table and a Bato Object.

### Approach, expanded

The owner was buying a first home and had a budget to spread over a year. We designed the whole unit at once, so every later purchase fits the plan. The design book holds the layout, the palette, and a specification for each room.

The sourcing list has three stages. Stage one is the sofa, a lamp, and a rug, which make the living room usable at once. Stage two adds the oat Unan Bed and Bakal Side Table; stage three adds the ebonized Pasilyo entry console and the balcony pieces. Natural mahogany, ebonized mahogany, and pale hinoki are mixed on purpose. The owner buys when ready, and nothing needs replacing along the way.
