## Lilim House

- **Location:** Antipolo, Rizal
- **Type:** Residential, family house
- **Floor area:** 320 sqm
- **Service:** Full-Service Furnishing
- **Year:** 2025
- **Scope:** Living, dining, four bedrooms, covered terrace
- **Brief:** A family of five moved into a newly built hillside house with deep eaves and large openings. They wanted rooms that stay cool and calm and that hold up to children.
- **Approach:** The studio kept furniture low so the views stay open. Cane and rattan let air move through the rooms. Dark wood grounds the bright, shaded light. Washable linen covers and wool rugs handle daily family use.
- **Material palette:** Plantation mahogany, cane, oat linen, wool, honed Romblon marble, unlacquered brass
- **Shop the project:** Silong Sofa, Dapithapon Lounge Chair, Batis Coffee Table, Hapag Dining Table, Hain Dining Chair, Himlay Bed, Aparador Cabinet, Lambot Wool Rug, Kapis Pendant, Tapayan Vessel
- **Image notes:** Shaded daylight, greenery through wide openings, long horizontal views, a terrace with a bench

### Approach, expanded

The house faces the valley and has deep eaves, so the rooms stay shaded and cool. We kept furniture low and left clear sightlines to the garden from the living room, the dining room, and the terrace. Cane panels and open weaves let air move through the house without fans running all day.

A family of five uses every room, so we chose pieces that can take it. Linen covers come off and wash. Wool rugs hide wear. Solid mahogany can be refinished when it marks. We placed the dining table in the middle of the plan because the family eats together there every day.

## Tanaw Residence

- **Location:** Bonifacio Global City, Taguig
- **Type:** Residential, two-bedroom condo
- **Floor area:** 118 sqm
- **Service:** Full-Service Furnishing
- **Year:** 2025
- **Scope:** Living and dining, two bedrooms, study
- **Brief:** A couple received a bare unit on a high floor. Both work long hours. They wanted to move in once, with everything in place.
- **Approach:** Bleached acacia and sand linen run through the whole unit so the small plan reads as one space. Storage is tall and closed. Lighting is low and warm, to contrast with the city outside the glass.
- **Material palette:** Bleached acacia, sand linen, terrazzo, blackened steel, brass, opal glass, wool
- **Shop the project:** Yakap Sofa (two-seat, sand), Salo Dining Table (Ø110), Hain Dining Chair (bleached acacia, four), Unan Bed (queen, sand), Bakal Side Table, Sulok Side Table (bleached), Estante Shelving (bleached shelves), Baul Credenza (bleached), Sinag Floor Lamp, Luad Table Lamp, Tala Wall Light, Hablon Cushion, Amihan No. 3
- **Image notes:** City skyline at dusk through full-height windows, warm lamps, compact rooms, tidy surfaces

### Approach, expanded

The unit is 118 sqm, and the plan is compact. We used one wood tone, bleached acacia, and one linen color, sand, in every room. Floors and joinery share that pale finish; black comes only from steel. The fully upholstered Yakap and Unan soften the stone and metal pieces. The eye does not stop at a change of material, so the unit reads as one larger space.

Both owners work long hours and come home late. We kept storage tall and closed so surfaces stay clear. Lamps sit low and give a warm light that contrasts with the glass and the skyline. Each room has one place to put a bag, a book, and a glass of water.

## Dalampasigan Inn

- **Location:** San Juan, La Union
- **Type:** Hospitality, 12-room boutique inn
- **Floor area:** 640 sqm
- **Service:** Full-Service Furnishing
- **Year:** 2024
- **Scope:** Twelve guest rooms, lobby, breakfast room
- **Brief:** The owners finished building a small inn near the beach. They needed twelve rooms and two shared spaces ready for guests in one season, with pieces that survive salt air and sand.
- **Approach:** The studio designed one room type and repeated it with small changes in textile color. Furniture is natural oiled acacia, honey in tone, solid and repairable. Reed mats replace wool rugs because they handle sand. The breakfast room has one long shared table.
- **Material palette:** Natural oiled acacia (honey), tikog reed, rattan, handloomed cotton, stoneware, limewash
- **Shop the project:** Himlay Bed (acacia), Bangko Bench, Tuntungan Stool, Banig Rug, Hain Dining Chair (acacia), Hapag Dining Table (260 cm, acacia), Lenta Pendant, Kumot Throw, Bilao Tray, Habi Wall Piece

### Approach, expanded

The owners needed twelve rooms ready in one season. We designed one room and repeated it, changing only the textile color from room to room. This shortened the schedule and made each room easy for staff to prepare and service.

Salt, sand, and heavy use shape every choice. The Himlay, Hain, and Hapag share the honey tone of natural oiled acacia. Furniture is solid wood that can be sanded, oiled, and repaired. Tikog reed mats clean easily. In the breakfast room, one long table lets guests sit together or apart.

## Pahinga Villas

- **Location:** Panglao, Bohol
- **Type:** Hospitality, 8-villa resort
- **Floor area:** 520 sqm
- **Service:** Full-Service Furnishing
- **Year:** 2025
- **Scope:** Eight one-bedroom villas, reception pavilion
- **Brief:** The owners built eight villas in a coconut grove a short walk from the beach. They wanted each villa to feel like a private house, not a hotel room, and to stay easy to maintain between guests.
- **Approach:** Each villa has one generous bed, one daybed on the terrace, and very little else. Cane and rattan keep air moving when the doors are open. White linen curtains soften the strong island light. The studio specified ebonized wood and white linen across all eight villas so any piece can be repaired or replaced from stock.
- **Material palette:** Ebonized plantation mahogany, honey rattan and cane, white linen, honed cream limestone, stoneware, capiz shell
- **Shop the project:** Himlay Bed (ebonized mahogany), Duyan Daybed, Dapithapon Lounge Chair, Sulok Side Table (ebonized), Kapis Pendant, Luad Table Lamp, Tabing Linen Curtain, Tapayan Vessel, Habi Wall Piece
- **Image notes:** Filtered light through coconut palms, open sliding doors, a low bed with white linen, a daybed on a shaded terrace, warm lamps at dusk

### Approach, expanded

Each villa has one generous bed, one daybed on the terrace, and very little else. We wanted guests to feel they were in a private house, so the rooms hold only what people use. Doors open to the grove, and cane and rattan keep air moving through the villa.

The owners need the villas to stay easy to run. We specified ebonized wood and white linen for all eight villas. If a piece wears or breaks, staff can repair it or replace it from a single stock. Frames, posts, rafters, and built-ins are ebonized mahogany above honed cream limestone floors. Natural honey rattan stays light against the dark wood. The reception pavilion uses the same materials, so the property reads as one place.

## Hardin Apartment

- **Location:** Legazpi Village, Makati
- **Type:** Residential, one-bedroom condo
- **Floor area:** 74 sqm
- **Service:** Interior Design Direction
- **Year:** 2026
- **Scope:** Full unit design book, renders of living room and bedroom
- **Brief:** A first-time owner wanted a clear plan to buy toward over a year, starting with a few good pieces.
- **Approach:** The studio delivered a layout, a palette, and a phased sourcing list in three stages. The owner bought the sofa, a lamp, and a rug first. Later stages add the oat Unan Bed, Bakal Side Table, and ebonized Pasilyo. Natural mahogany, ebonized mahogany, and hinoki are mixed on purpose.
- **Material palette:** Natural and ebonized plantation mahogany, clay and oat linen, cane, stoneware, hinoki
- **Shop the project:** Silong Sofa (two-seat, clay), Kiso Stool, Luad Table Lamp, Lambot Wool Rug (170 × 240), Pasilyo Console Table (ebonized mahogany), Bato Object, Unan Bed (queen, oat), Bakal Side Table
- **Image notes:** Mix renders and photographs. Show the design book pages, a moodboard, and a floor plan beside finished-room images. Plants and a small balcony garden.

### Gallery captions

### Approach, expanded

The owner was buying a first home and had a budget to spread over a year. We designed the whole unit at once, so every later purchase fits the plan. The design book holds the layout, the palette, and a specification for each room.

The sourcing list has three stages. Stage one is the sofa, a lamp, and a rug, which make the living room usable at once. Stage two adds the oat Unan Bed and Bakal Side Table; stage three adds the ebonized Pasilyo entry console and the balcony pieces. Natural mahogany, ebonized mahogany, and pale hinoki are mixed on purpose. The owner buys when ready, and nothing needs replacing along the way.
