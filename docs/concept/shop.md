# Shop

## Shop direction

Status: **Locked by Aedwon.** This covers every section down to and including "Collections."

- **Shop sourcing model:** Mixed assortment of Kanlungan-original products and curated third-party pieces
- **Curated maker sourcing:** Philippine-first and predominantly Philippine-made or Philippine-designed, with a smaller, selective international assortment when it meaningfully complements Kanlungan's point of view
- **Shop positioning:** Small, highly curated, quality-first, sophisticated, and intentionally selective rather than broad or department-store-like

Kanlungan's shop is a small, highly curated collection of sophisticated, high-quality pieces. The assortment should feel edited by an interior studio, not like a general furniture marketplace.

The shop may combine signature Kanlungan-designed pieces with curated third-party products from fictional makers and partners. Every product should fit the warm modern Filipino direction and be credible for use in Kanlungan's own furnishing projects.

### Curated makers

Curated makers should be predominantly Philippine-first. Kanlungan should favor Philippine designers, craftspeople, studios, and makers as the core of the third-party assortment, while allowing a smaller number of international makers when their work adds something distinctive and remains compatible with the brand.

This is not a Philippine-only rule. The intent is to make local design and craft the dominant voice without artificially excluding strong international pieces that fit the collection.

### Assortment rules

A product belongs in the Kanlungan shop only when it satisfies the brand's curation standard:

- **Design fit:** It aligns with the warm modern Filipino direction.
- **Material quality:** Favor natural or honest materials such as wood, stone, clay, woven fibers, linen, metal, and glass with considered finishes.
- **Durability:** It should feel suitable for long-term residential or boutique hospitality use.
- **Craftsmanship:** Construction, joinery, finish, and detailing should feel intentional and premium.
- **Restraint:** Avoid trend-chasing, novelty pieces, loud branding, and conspicuous luxury.
- **Project credibility:** Kanlungan should be able to plausibly specify the piece in one of its own furnishing projects.
- **Distinctiveness:** Every product needs a clear reason to exist; do not add near-duplicate filler simply to make the catalog appear larger.
- **Maker transparency:** Curated products should clearly identify the maker, origin, materials, and relevant craft story.
- **Kanlungan originals:** Kanlungan-designed pieces should represent the clearest expression of the brand and may be exclusive, configurable, or made-to-order.
- **Curation priority:** Philippine makers should dominate the third-party assortment, with selective international pieces included only when they add something meaningfully distinct.

A useful internal test is: **Would Kanlungan confidently specify this piece in one of its own finished spaces?** If not, it does not belong in the shop.

The shop should explicitly avoid becoming a general home-goods retailer. Exclude generic mass-market furniture, major appliances, electronics, mattresses, plumbing fixtures, built-ins, construction materials, and broad kitchenware or homeware catalogs.

### Product depth

Product depth should be relative rather than quota-driven. Do not set an arbitrary total SKU target and do not create filler simply to make the catalog look larger.

- **Anchor categories — deepest:** Seating, Tables, Lighting
- **Supporting categories — moderate:** Beds, Storage, Rugs and Textiles
- **Accent/discovery categories — flexible:** Decor and Objects, Art

A product should only be added when it contributes a meaningfully different function, scale, silhouette, material, maker, price position, or spatial use. Subcategories do not need to be populated evenly or immediately; they should only appear publicly when there is enough credible assortment to feel intentional.

Kanlungan Originals should be visibly present across the major categories as signature pieces, but there is no required percentage split between originals and curated maker products.

### Product information standard

Every product should include only the essential information needed to feel credible and purchasable:

- Product name
- One-line description
- Price
- Maker or **Kanlungan Original** attribution
- Materials
- Dimensions
- Origin
- Lead time or stock status
- Care information only when relevant

More detailed storytelling, specifications, or provenance can be added when a specific product genuinely benefits from them, but they are not mandatory across the catalog.

Product photography direction will be defined later as part of the visual system and imagery rules.

### Collections

**Collections** is the customer-facing term for curated groups of products organized around a coherent room, mood, material story, or way of living rather than a product type. Products in a collection remain individually purchasable unless explicitly sold together.

Kanlungan will use three collection types:

1. **Room-based collections — primary.** Practical collections such as Living Room, Dining Room, and Bedroom that help customers shop by space and furnishing need.
2. **Mood and lifestyle collections — secondary.** More expressive collections such as Quiet Living, Slow Mornings, or Soft Evenings that communicate the atmosphere Kanlungan is trying to create.
3. **Material-based collections — selective.** Focused collections such as Natural Wood, Woven Forms, or Stone and Clay that highlight a coherent material language.

A product may belong to multiple collections when appropriate. Room-based collections should do most of the navigational work, while mood/lifestyle and material collections should be used more selectively to express Kanlungan's point of view.

Seasonal collections are not a priority because they would push the brand toward a faster, retail-driven merchandising cadence that does not fit the intended premium interiors positioning.

Collections exist to demonstrate Kanlungan's taste and furnishing perspective, help customers see how pieces work together, and connect the shop more naturally to the studio's furnishing service.

The term **bundle** should be reserved only for cases where multiple products are actually packaged and sold together as one purchasable set. Do not use “editorial collection” as customer-facing language.

## Availability model

Status: **Decided on delegation.**

The shop is mixed.

- **Made to order:** furniture, Kanlungan Originals, large rugs, curtains, and large wall pieces. Each product shows a lead time in weeks.
- **In stock:** most lighting, cushions, throws, decor and objects, and prints. These ship in 3 to 7 business days.

Every product shows exactly one of these states:

| State | Shown as | Can be bought |
|---|---|---|
| In stock | "In stock. Ships in 3 to 7 business days." | Yes |
| Made to order | "Made to order. Ready in 8 weeks." | Yes |
| Sold out | "Sold out." with a "Notify me" email field | No |

One-of-a-kind pieces, such as original paintings, show "One of a kind" next to the state. There is no pre-order state and no "low stock" urgency message.

## Price positioning

Status: **Decided on delegation.**

Premium, below international luxury brands. Prices reflect solid materials, local craft, and small production runs. The shop never competes on price.

Rules:

- Prices are in Philippine pesos, VAT included, in whole pesos.
- One price per product or variant. No discounts, sales, promo codes, or strike-through prices.
- No "price on request" in the shop. A piece with no fixed price belongs to the studio service, not the shop.

## Variants and customization

Status: **Decided on delegation.**

- A product may offer at most two option types: **finish or material**, and **size**. Upholstered pieces offer **fabric** in place of finish.
- Each option type has two to four choices. Choices are shown as swatches or labels, not as a dropdown.
- A variant may change the price and the lead time.
- There is no free-form customization in the shop. Each made-to-order product page carries one line: "Need a different size or finish? Ask the studio." It links to the contact form.
- Curtains are sold per panel in fixed sizes. Made-to-measure curtains belong to the studio service.

## Links between the shop and the studio

Status: **Decided on delegation.**

- **Shop the project.** Each project page lists the shop products used in that project.
- **Seen in.** A product page shows the projects that used the product, when any exist.
- **Furnishing prompt.** Collection pages and the cart show one quiet prompt for Full-Service Furnishing. In the cart, it appears only when the cart holds four or more furniture pieces.
- **Maker pages.** Each maker has a short page with their story and their products.

There is no trade program and no loyalty program.

## Delivery and returns

Status: **Decided on delegation.**

- **Area:** Philippines only. International orders go through the contact form.
- **Furniture:** white-glove delivery with placement and assembly. Free in Metro Manila for orders above ₱50,000, otherwise ₱2,500. Outside Metro Manila, the fee is calculated at checkout by region.
- **Small items:** courier delivery nationwide. Free above ₱10,000, otherwise ₱350.
- **Returns:** in-stock items can be returned within 14 days, unused. Made-to-order and one-of-a-kind items cannot be returned, except for defects or transit damage.
- **Warranty:** two years on furniture construction for Kanlungan Originals. Maker products carry the maker's warranty.
