# Services

## Service direction

Status: **Locked by Aedwon.**

Kanlungan will stay intentionally focused on exactly two services.

### Full-Service Furnishing

For already-built, largely empty homes, condos, and hospitality spaces. Kanlungan takes the space from unfurnished to move-in or guest-ready without construction work.

Scope may include:

- Furniture
- Lighting and decorative fixtures
- Rugs, curtains, and textiles
- Art and decor
- Appliances and accessories where appropriate
- Sourcing and procurement
- Delivery and installation
- Final styling

This is a core customer-facing service. It is intentionally separate from construction, renovation, or design-build work.

### Design-Only Interior Package

Customer-facing name: **Interior Design Direction**.

For clients who want Kanlungan to design the space but will handle purchasing, installation, and any contractor work themselves.

Scope may include:

- Space planning and furniture layouts
- Moodboards and material palettes
- Color and lighting direction
- Furniture and fixture recommendations
- 3D renders of key rooms
- Room-by-room specifications
- Curated shopping and sourcing list
- Defined revision rounds

This service provides the design system and visual direction without execution.

## What Kanlungan does not do

Status: **Decided on delegation.** It follows from the locked service direction.

The site states this plainly on both service pages so the wrong inquiries do not arrive:

- No construction, renovation, or demolition
- No built-in cabinetry, plumbing, or electrical work
- No architectural design

If a space needs this work, the client completes it before Kanlungan starts.

## Process

Status: **Decided on delegation.**

### Full-Service Furnishing

1. **Discovery call.** A free 30-minute call about the space, the brief, and the budget.
2. **Site visit and proposal.** The studio measures the space and sends a fixed design fee and a furnishing budget range.
3. **Design.** Layouts, material palette, and a full furnishing plan. Two revision rounds.
4. **Procurement.** The studio orders every piece and manages makers and lead times.
5. **Installation and styling.** The crew delivers, installs, and styles the space, usually within one week on site.
6. **Handover.** A walkthrough, a care guide, and a 30-day follow-up visit.

Typical duration: 12 to 20 weeks from signed proposal to handover. Made-to-order lead times set most of that.

### Interior Design Direction

1. **Discovery call.** A free 30-minute call.
2. **Brief and measurements.** The client supplies floor plans and photos, or books a paid site measure.
3. **Concept.** Moodboard, material palette, and layout options.
4. **Design development.** Final layouts, renders of key rooms, and specifications. Two revision rounds.
5. **Delivery.** A design book and a sourcing list. One follow-up call within 60 days.

Typical duration: 5 to 8 weeks.

## Pricing model

Status: **Decided on delegation.**

The site shows starting prices. It does not show a full rate card. Exact fees come in a proposal.

### Full-Service Furnishing

- A fixed design and management fee, quoted per project and based on floor area and scope
- The furnishing budget is separate. The client pays for the pieces at listed prices. Kanlungan adds no hidden markup on shop items.
- Stated on the site as: "Furnishing projects start at ₱1,500,000, including the design fee."
- Payment: 50 percent of the design fee on signing, 50 percent at design approval. Furnishing budget: 70 percent before procurement, 30 percent before installation.

### Interior Design Direction

- Priced by floor area
- Stated on the site as: "From ₱2,500 per square meter. Minimum fee ₱150,000."
- Payment: 50 percent on signing, 50 percent before delivery of the design book.

### Discovery call

Free, 30 minutes, by video or at the Makati showroom.

## Service area

Status: **Decided on delegation.**

- Full-Service Furnishing: anywhere in the Philippines. Projects outside Metro Manila and Calabarzon carry travel and freight costs, stated in the proposal.
- Interior Design Direction: anywhere, because it is delivered remotely.

Status: **Decided on delegation.**

- Sourcing lists and furnishing plans draw on the shop first, then on outside sources.
- The shop presents Full-Service Furnishing as the next step for customers who are furnishing a whole room. See `docs/concept/shop.md`.
