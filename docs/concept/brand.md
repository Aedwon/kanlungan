# Brand

## Core decisions

Status: **Locked by Aedwon.**

- **Brand name:** Kanlungan
- **Business type:** Premium interior design studio with a secondary curated furniture and homeware shop
- **Primary business priority:** Studio first, shop second
- **Market focus:** Premium residential interiors and boutique hospitality
- **Design direction:** Warm modern Filipino
- **Filipino identity:** Subtle rather than overt; expressed through materials, craftsmanship, climate-aware design, spatial sensibility, and project context instead of decorative cultural motifs
- **Brand idea:** Kanlungan should evoke refuge, safety, rest, belonging, and spaces people want to return to
- **Commerce role:** The shop supports the studio brand rather than replacing it
- **Portfolio intent:** Demonstrate both high-end editorial/brand design and credible e-commerce functionality using a fictional business
- **Imagery:** Product and project imagery may be AI-generated because this is a demo brand, not an operating business

## Name

Status: **Decided on delegation.**

"Kanlungan" is a Filipino word for shelter or refuge. The site explains the meaning once, briefly, on the About page. It does not translate or explain the word elsewhere.

Write the name as `Kanlungan`. Do not set it in all caps in running text. The logo treatment is open.

## Tagline

Status: **Decided on delegation.**

**Spaces to come home to.**

Supporting line for longer contexts: "Kanlungan is an interior design studio and shop for homes and small hotels that feel like refuge."

## Audience

Status: **Decided on delegation.**

- **Homeowners.** Owners of newly turned-over condos and houses in Metro Manila and nearby provinces. Aged 30 to 55. They have budget and taste but little time. They want a finished home without managing many vendors.
- **Hospitality owners.** Owners of boutique inns, small resorts, and serviced residences of roughly 5 to 30 rooms. They want rooms that photograph well and survive guest use.
- **Design-led shoppers.** People who follow the studio's work and buy one considered piece at a time. Some later become studio clients.

## Fictional business facts

Status: **Decided on delegation.**

Use these consistently wherever the site needs them.

- Founded 2019
- Studio and showroom in Makati City, Metro Manila. The showroom opens by appointment.
- Team of 12: designers, a procurement lead, and an installation crew
- Works across the Philippines. Most projects are in Metro Manila, Calabarzon, Cebu, and coastal Luzon.
- Email `studio@kanlungan.example`. Phone numbers are placeholders in the form `+63 2 8555 01XX`.

Do not name a founder. Refer to "the studio" or "our designers." This avoids inventing a person who could be mistaken for a real one.

## Voice

Status: **Decided on delegation.** Full rules and key copy are in `docs/content/copy.md`.

Calm, plain, specific, and warm. The studio sounds like a confident designer talking to a client, not like an advertisement.
